FrameTrail: one window, four sections

Preview / Analysis / Playback / Camera remain in the shared sidebar.
Ctrl+1, Ctrl+2, Ctrl+3, Ctrl+4 switch sections.
Analysis and preparation can continue in the background; the top badge shows activity.
Playback pauses while hidden. Leaving Camera requests capture to stop.

Playback: choose a video or copy the Preview video/settings, then Prepare playback.
Quitting preserves the Save/Discard/Cancel choice for unsaved analyses.

Run-FrameTrail.cmd uses the unchanged YOLOX-S default.
Run-Balanced-Preview.cmd selects the unchanged held RF preview.
See REPORT.md for verification scope and screenshots.
